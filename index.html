BCMS - Emergency Disruption Reporting & Broadcast System

Restructuring the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) to accelerate disaster response, automate regional corporate email notifications, and enforce strict role-based access controls across nationwide branches.

Organization

PT Pegadaian

Role

IT Business Analyst

Regulatory Target

POJK No. 4 / 2021

Impact Scope

Nationwide Network (HQ, Kanwil, & Area Consols)

Executive Overview & Background

Developing and expanding the Incident and Disaster Management Module within the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) serves as a critical operational tool to accelerate emergency response during crisis events. This initiative ensures business continuity while fulfilling regulatory compliance under POJK No. 4 / 2021 regarding Integrated Risk Management in Information Technology for Non-Bank Financial Institutions.

Key Business Objectives

  • Rapid Incident Reporting: Provide a standardized media platform to instantly report disaster events and operational disruptions at any work unit/branch.
  • Automated Broadcast Notifications: Raise immediate situational awareness by delivering automated emergency alerts directly to personal corporate emails of affected regional leaders and personnel.
  • Impact & Damage Assessment: Enable tracking of asset damage, facility downtime, equipment status, and recovery time objectives (RTO).
  • Monitoring & Recovery Action Plan: Evaluate post-incident recovery actions, track operational suspension durations, and calculate recovery costs under Crisis Management Team governance.

Process Re-Engineering (As-Is vs To-Be)

As-Is Workflow

Standard reporting notified higher-tier organizational emails sequentially, causing potential delays in multi-tier regional escalation during critical disaster situations.

To-Be Workflow

Integrated module restructuring featuring sub-modules: Dashboard, Initial Report, Follow-Up, Monitoring, Reports, and a dedicated Emergency Disruption Announcement system.

My Role & Technical BA Deliverables

  • Business Requirement Document (BRD) Compilation: Translated user needs into structured functional requirements, including UI module rearrangement and navigation logic.
  • User Access Matrix (UAM) Specification: Formulated strict access privileges for new operational roles, introducing Super Announcer (HQ & Admin) and Announcer (Reviewer & Approver BCM) roles.
  • Notification Routing Logic & Parameters: Designed functional parameters allowing dynamic mapping of target corporate email recipients across nationwide Consol units (12 Regional Offices & 70+ Area Consols).
  • Cross-Functional Alignment & SDLC Support: Facilitated requirement clarifications and guided development through SIT, UAT, Regression Testing, and Deployment phases with IT PMO, Risk Management Users, Developers, and QA teams.
