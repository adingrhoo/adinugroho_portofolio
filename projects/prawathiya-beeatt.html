Executive Overview & Background

Commissioned directly by the Board of Directors, this initiative focused on re-engineering the legacy internal HRIS and attendance app ("Beeatt"). The legacy architecture (Native Mobile + CodeIgniter Web) suffered from sluggish performance, high maintenance overhead, and unconstrained permit approvals. Additionally, employees assigned to client sites lacked a verified mechanism to log attendance, while legacy approval queues frequently stalled without operational time limits.

Key Business Objectives

  • Full Tech Stack Modernization: Re-engineer legacy Native Mobile code to cross-platform Flutter and migrate backend PHP CodeIgniter to Laravel to boost system responsiveness and maintainability.
  • Geofencing & Anti-Fake GPS Security: Restrict office clock-ins within a strict 100-meter radius using corporate email authentication and robust anti-fake GPS location verification.
  • Dynamic On-Site Client Location Approval: Enable field and outsourced staff visiting client sites for the first time to submit location coordinates (Lat/Long) and live photos for HR verification and approval.
  • Automated 3-Day Manager SLA & Annual Reset: Implement a strict 3-day SLA auto-rejection timer for manager permit approvals and an automated annual leave quota reset engine running every January 1st.
  • Comprehensive Permit & Admin Portal: Digitally streamline submissions for sick leave (with medical note upload), late arrivals, early exits, and overtime alongside web-based organizational setup and reporting export tools.

Process Re-Engineering (As-Is vs To-Be)

As-Is Workflow (Legacy Stack)

Sluggish Native Mobile and CodeIgniter web architecture, vulnerable location tracking without spoofing protection, manual location additions for client visits, and unmonitored approval queues that stagnated indefinitely.

To-Be Workflow (Re-Engineered)

High-performance Flutter Mobile & Laravel Web platform featuring anti-fake GPS geofencing, photo + Lat/Long dynamic client site onboarding, and auto-reject 3-day SLA timers for mobile managers.

System Solution & Scope

Anti-Spoofing Geofenced Clock-In

Enforces corporate email login, 100m office geofence limits, and location spoofing detection to guarantee authentic clock-in/out timestamps.

Dynamic Client Site Registration

Empowers field staff to register new client visit locations by capturing live photos and Longitude/Latitude payload for instant HR approval.

3-Day SLA & Mobile Approvals

Allows managers to approve leave/overtime requests directly from mobile, with an automated 3-day SLA countdown that auto-rejects inactive requests.

Automated Leave Reset & Admin Web

Executes an annual leave reset scheduler every January 1st while providing HR admins with organizational management and exportable attendance reports.
