Executive Overview & Background

PT. Sinar Mentari Makmur (SMM Leasing) required an attendance and workforce system specifically tailored for their operational structure. SMM executive leadership requested a dedicated, standalone application instance separate from the core multi-tenant Beeatt platform. Furthermore, SMM's mobile workforce comprises field collectors and survey agents who constantly travel to client sites for debt collection and asset repossession—making fixed office geofencing impractical. Simultaneously, Head Office operations required seamless attendance data feeds directly into the internal office payroll system.

Key Business Objectives

  • Client-Mandated Isolated Instance: Deliver a standalone application environment dedicated to SMM Leasing, fulfilling client requirements for isolated branding and database boundaries.
  • Location-Unconstrained Mobile Clock-In for Collectors: Bypass fixed geofence restrictions for field collector profiles to enable seamless mobile clock-ins/outs during field surveys and collection visits.
  • Streamlined Collector Interface: De-clutter the mobile experience for field collectors by removing unnecessary permit, leave, and sick request modules.
  • Head Office Payroll REST API Integration: Bridge Head Office attendance records seamlessly with the internal corporate payroll engine via REST API to automate monthly salary processing.

Process Re-Engineering (As-Is vs To-Be)

As-Is Workflow (Standard Beeatt Model)

Single multi-tenant platform with rigid 100m geofencing that blocked mobile field collectors on client visits, combined with manual data extraction workarounds for Head Office payroll processing.

To-Be Workflow (SMM Custom Instance)

Dedicated white-label instance with location-free mobile clock-ins for field collectors, paired with automated REST API attendance payload sync into the internal Head Office payroll system.
