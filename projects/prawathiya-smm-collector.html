Executive Overview & Background

Field collectors at PT. Sinar Mentari Makmur (SMM Leasing) manage critical operations including field debt collection, motor vehicle & home appliance asset repossessions, and initial borrower creditworthiness surveys. Historically, daily task assignments were distributed manually, survey evaluations (income, existing debt, neighborhood checks) lacked structured verification, and collector fee tracking was prone to dispute. SMM required a unified digital platform to govern collector certifications, automate regional task scheduling, and streamline fee disbursements.

Key Business Objectives

  • Mandatory Collector Certification Governance: Maintain complete collector profiles on Web Admin, enforcing tracking for compulsory professional certification credentials required for debt collection.
  • Automated 4:00 AM Daily Task Auto-Sync: Implement a regional task assignment engine capping daily tasks at 5–10 items per collector, automatically syncing to mobile devices every morning at 4:00 AM.
  • Structured Survey & Repossession Protocols: Standardize mobile evaluations for new loan applicants (income verification, debt profiling, residence condition, community background checks) and asset repossessions (motorcycles & appliances).
  • Two-Tier Task Verification & Auto-Fee Credit: Enable Admin verification of completed field tasks; approved tasks credit collector mobile balances automatically, while rejected tasks trigger push notifications demanding next-day re-validation.
  • Fee Claim & Admin Disbursement Workflow: Establish a transparent ledger system where accumulated task fees are reviewed and approved by Admin prior to direct bank transfer disbursement.

Process Re-Engineering (As-Is vs To-Be)

As-Is Workflow (Manual Operation)

Uncoordinated task distribution via messaging apps, unverified collector regulatory certifications, subjective paper-based credit surveys, and manual spreadsheet-based fee calculations.

To-Be Workflow (Digitized System)

Automated 4:00 AM mobile task sync, centralized certification tracking, objective mobile survey checklist engines, and automated task fee crediting with formal admin payout approval.

System Solution & Scope

Web Admin Dispatch & Certification Portal

Allows admins to manage certified collector directories, upload daily regional collection/survey tasks, verify submitted proof, and approve fee disbursement payouts.

4:00 AM Auto-Sync Mobile Engine

Wakes mobile apps every morning at 4:00 AM to fetch freshly assigned daily field tasks (capped at 5–10 tasks per collector based on regional jurisdiction).

Field Credit Survey & Repossession UX

Guides collectors through structured applicant evaluation forms (income, debts, house condition, neighbor feedback) and asset seizure documentation for motor vehicles and appliances.

Collector Fee Ledger & Claim Request

Logs earned fees transparently inside the mobile app upon task approval and allows collectors to submit formal withdrawal requests to HR/Finance.
