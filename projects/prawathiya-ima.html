Executive Overview & Background

Indonesia Marketing Association (IMA) is a national organization governing thousands of marketing professionals across regional chapters throughout Indonesia. Prior to this greenfield initiative, membership operations, registrations, active status validations, and event distributions were managed entirely through fragmented, manual spreadsheet processes across individual chapter leaderships.

Key Business Objectives

  • Centralized Digital Registration: Replace manual Excel tracking with a self-service Mobile Application (App Store & Play Store) for direct applicant onboardings.
  • Multi-Chapter Decentralized Approval: Route member applications automatically to respective regional chapter leads (e.g., Bogor, Jakarta) for localized verification.
  • Inter-Chapter Member Mutation Workflow: Establish formal request and dual-approval mechanisms when members transfer between regional chapters.
  • Real-Time Event & Article Sync: Enable Central & Chapter Admins to publish promotional event banners on the Web Dashboard that instantaneously sync to mobile feeds.

Process Re-Engineering (As-Is vs To-Be)

As-Is Workflow (Legacy)

Manual spreadsheet management per chapter, uncoordinated registration approvals via physical messaging, lost membership histories during relocation, and unstandardized event announcements via chat groups.

To-Be Workflow (Target)

Unified Web & Mobile ecosystem with auto-routed regional approvals, transparent dual-check mutation workflows, centralized relational DB, and instant cross-platform banner notifications.

System Solution & Scope

Cross-Platform Mobile App

Allows prospective members to apply directly, receive approval notifications, access articles, and request chapter transfers from iOS & Android devices.

Web Admin Dashboard

Centralized portal for Chapter Leads and Super Admins to review applications, toggle active/inactive statuses, publish articles, and host events.

Multi-Chapter Hierarchy

Structured data partitioning ensuring regional chapter heads (e.g., Bogor) strictly access and manage applicants within their designated jurisdiction.

Real-Time Banner Sync

Automated integration pushing event updates created on the Web Dashboard directly to member mobile app home banners.
