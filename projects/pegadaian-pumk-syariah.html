PUMK Syariah Working Capital Flow Enhancement

Streamlining working capital application workflows, integrating multi-BUMN fund allocations, and updating approval authority matrix based on new SOTK governance at PT Pegadaian.

ORGANIZATION PT Pegadaian
ROLE IT Business Analyst
REGULATORY TARGET BUMN Circular & SAK EP Compliance
IMPACT SCOPE Multi-Tier Network (HQ, Regional, & Outlets)

Executive Overview & Background

Developing and expanding the PUMK Syariah (Islamic Micro and Small Business Funding) Working Capital Module at PT Pegadaian aims to accelerate operational workflows for partner business funding requests. This initiative restructures multi-tier funding allocations while enforcing financial compliance under BUMN Circular Letters and updated SAK EP accounting standards.

Key Business Objectives

  • SLA Turnaround Acceleration: Eliminate manual bureaucracy and physical document dependencies, cutting turnaround time from 2–3 business days down to rapid digital processing.
  • Accounting Compliance (SAK EP): Transition financial reporting standards from SAK ETAP to SAK EP and establish full audit transparency for multi-BUMN fund sources.
  • New SOTK Approval Alignment: Reconfigure loan approval tiering matrix in compliance with updated organizational governance structure (SOTK).
  • Financial Journal Automation: Ensure automated, error-free accounting journal generation across fund allocation, dropping, disbursement, and early settlement.

Process Re-Engineering (As-Is vs To-Be)

As-Is Workflow (Legacy)

Working capital application and fund source flagging were bundled into manual per-transaction requests via e-office. Requires extensive multi-unit physical file routing with 2–3 days SLA turnaround.

To-Be Workflow (Target)

Decoupled BUMN Mapping menu and multi-tier Fund Allocation (HQ → Regional → Branch). Features automated journal simulations, SSO integration, and rapid branch disbursement clearance.

My Role & Technical BA Deliverables

  • Business Requirement Document (BRD) Compilation: Translated business needs into structured functional specifications, including UI mockup rearrangement and navigation logic.
  • BUMN Mapping & Flagging Rules: Formulated functional specifications for mapping multi-BUMN funding sources per unit, locking automated journaling across cash and manual entries.
  • SOTK Approval Matrix Configuration: Re-architected loan approval thresholds (Assistant Manager for IDR 1M–20M and Business Manager for > IDR 20M).
  • Journal Logic & SSO Validation: Designed automated accounting simulation flows (dropping, disbursement, early settlement) and specified integrated SSO authentication logic.
