G-Law Integrated Contract System

Enhancing end-to-end legal review workflows, expanding dynamic partner legal document requirements, and centralizing Akad Wakalah repository under SE Direksi No. 217/SE/2025 at PT Pegadaian.

ORGANIZATION PT Pegadaian
ROLE IT Business Analyst
REGULATORY TARGET SE Direksi No. 217/SE/2025 & OJK Compliance
IMPACT SCOPE Enterprise Network (HQ, Regional, & Area Units)

Executive Overview & Background

Enforcing Board Circular Letter No. 217/SE/2025 mandated the use of the G-Law Integrated Contract System across all head office, regional, and area operational units for contract review, signing, and archiving. This system enhancement project upgrades platform capabilities by streamlining legal review workflows, automating lifecycle email notifications, and creating dedicated Sharia repositories to minimize institutional legal exposure.

Key Business Objectives

  • Automated Legal Review Workflows: Streamline legal document evaluation, comment tracking, and revision flows between Legal staff and proposing business units.
  • Granular Counterparty Legality Matrix: Expand static partner categorizations to dynamic legal document requirements for CV, Firm, Foundation, University, Community, and Individual counterparties.
  • Automated Email Lifecycle Alerts: Establish automated email notifications for review completion, signed document uploads, contract renewal warnings, and review cancellations.
  • Centralized Sharia & Historical Repositories: Build a dedicated Akad Wakalah repository for Islamic Business Units to fulfill OJK compliance reporting and support pre-system historical contract digitization.

Process Re-Engineering (As-Is vs To-Be)

As-Is Workflow (Legacy)

Rigid legal review flows with limited "Other" partner categories, loss of review comments on exported files, unintegrated user roles, manual email follow-ups, and missing digital repositories for Wakalah agreements.

To-Be Workflow (Target)

End-to-end digital contract management linked with SSO Pegadaian. Features dynamic legal document uploads per partner type, automated email alerts, and specialized Wakalah repositories.

My Role & Technical BA Deliverables

  • Business Requirement Document (BRD) Compilation: Authored detailed functional specifications for Phase 1 & Phase 2 system enhancements across Review, Addendum, Repository, and Reporting modules.
  • Counterparty Legality Requirements Matrix: Defined explicit legal document upload rules and nomenclature for diverse partner types (Universities, CVs, Foundations, Partnerships).
  • Review Comment & Revision Tracking Rules: Specified functional requirements to ensure all legal review comments, notes, and revision history are accurately captured and preserved across exported documents.
  • SSO Sync & Email Lifecycle Notification Rules: Designed user role synchronization with SSO Pegadaian and drafted automated corporate email templates for contract lifecycle milestones.
