Passion PKP - Corporate Loss Recovery & Management System

REGULATORY TARGET Perdir No. 48/2024 & GCG Compliance

Executive Overview & Background

Developing the Penanganan Kerugian Perusahaan (PKP) module on the Passion core application establishes an integrated digital framework for recording, reporting, handling, and monitoring financial losses at PT Pegadaian. Driven by Board Regulation No. 48/2024, this initiative automates loss recovery tracking resulting from Unlawful Acts (PMH) or Breach of Contract (Wanprestasi) to uphold Good Corporate Governance (GCG) principles.

Key Business Objectives

  • Automated Cut-Off & Reclassification: Halt interest accruals (sewa modal/mu'nah) automatically and reclassify non-performing loans (PYD) into loss accounts (KPYD) upon internal audit findings (LHPP).
  • Legal Instrument Integration: Digitally capture Absolute Responsibility Statements (SKTM) for unlawful acts and Written Loss Statements (SPBJ) for defaults, linked with asset collateral mapping.
  • Batch Time-out Prevention: Re-engineer bulk journaling routines into single Decree (SK) executions with pre-execution simulation previews to eliminate system timeouts in GL posting.
  • Dual-Tier Validation & Recovery Tracking: Enforce strict governance between Branch Managers (Pinca) and Regional Deputy Directors (Deputy Bisnis) while tracking recovery channels (salary deductions, auctions, voluntary cash deposits).

Process Re-Engineering (As-Is vs To-Be)

As-Is Workflow (Legacy)

Loss handling relied on fragmented manual reports and unintegrated offline document transfers. Bulk GL journal posting caused frequent system timeouts, while recovery tracking lacked real-time visibility across branch networks.

To-Be Workflow (Target)

Fully digital PKP workflow in Passion core app featuring automated cut-off journaling, digital SKTM/SPBJ generation, per-SK journal simulation, dual approval controls, and multi-channel recovery tracking.

My Role & Technical BA Deliverables

  • Business Requirement Document (BRD) Compilation: Formulated end-to-end functional requirements for Phase 3 PKP module on Passion system covering Cut-Off, SKTM/SPBJ, Loss Imposition, and Reporting.
  • User Access Matrix (UAM) Specification: Architected strict role permissions defining Branch Manager actions (input, detail costs, journal execution) vs Deputy Business authority (credit list validation, SK approval, and loss deletion).
  • Accounting Journal & Simulation Logic: Engineered automated GL journal posting rules for auction deficits and TGR receivable creation, complete with pre-commit "Simulation Journal" validation.
  • Recovery Mechanism & De-registration Workflows: Designed multi-channel recovery algorithms (payroll deduction, cash setoran, auction proceeds) and TGR deletion logic for credit re-settlement clearance.
